# 鲸鱼娘（红色） / Whale Girl (Red)

`whale-girl-red` is a red-and-white recolor of `../whale-refined/`, bundled as
an independent selectable pet. The original and refined pets are retained.

鲸鱼娘（红色）是精致版的独立红白配色变体，原版与精致版均保留。

- Atlas: 1536 × 1872, eight columns and nine rows of 192 × 208 cells.
- Frames: 6 / 8 / 8 / 4 / 5 / 8 / 6 / 6 / 6 (57 total), with the refined pet's durations.
- Blue/navy/cyan hair, eyes, clothing and tail become red; white, skin tones and
  gold trim keep their original pixel values. The atlas alpha channel is identical.
- `previews/idle.gif` is generated from the six recolored idle cells at their
  manifest durations; GIF transparency is binary, while the WebP retains full alpha.
- Recolor: DescartHsu, using deterministic color replacement. The asset inherits
  the refined manifest's MIT declaration.

The refined design's source attribution is recorded in the repository's
[English README](../../README.md#built-in-pets) and
[Chinese README](../../README.zh.md#内置宠物): it references DreamSkin's
DeepSeek whale-girl theme, historically attributed to `powerdog996` under MIT.
This recolor preserves that source and derivative relationship; it does not
redefine the original artwork's licensing scope.
